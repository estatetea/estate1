# Brew promo — script (about 40 seconds)

**Goal:** someone who has never heard of us understands, in 40 seconds, what Estate Tea is, what Brew does *for them*, and exactly how to start (scan the code / message Brew on WhatsApp).

**Voice:** warm, natural female voice (Sulafat), recorded as one continuous read.
**Music:** dark, low opening under the tea pour that grows (no stop) into a driving cinematic score; a hit on every cut.

## How this captures a new visitor

1. **What Estate Tea is** — said in the first 3 seconds, over the product shot.
2. **Why they should care** — every section is about *them*: running low, ordering, reordering, a business skipping the calls.
3. **What to do next** — the last 6 seconds hold the QR code, the WhatsApp number and estatetea.in on screen while the voice says "Scan the code, and say hi to Brew."

**On the page itself:** put a **"Chat with Brew on WhatsApp"** button right under the video (the same link as the QR: `wa.me/919742668951?text=Hi%20Brew!`), so people on a phone can tap instead of scanning their own screen.
